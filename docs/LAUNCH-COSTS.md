# Small-launch cost estimate — September 9, 2026

This is a U.S. infrastructure estimate, not an all-in business budget or vendor quote. No service accounts or purchases have been made. Domain availability, taxes and final checkout prices must be confirmed.

- Render starter web service: $7/month. One GB persistent disk: $0.25/month. Base hosting: $7.25/month. https://render.com/pricing
- Standard .com at Porkbun: listed $11.08/year for registration and renewal when checked; premium domains may differ. https://porkbun.com/products/domains
- Clerk parent sign-in: free allowance includes 50,000 monthly retained users; features/limits and eligibility must be verified when selecting the plan. Child profiles are internal records, not individual Clerk accounts. https://clerk.com/pricing
- Manual signed consent review: no per-verification vendor charge; requires the operator's time. Resend is not required or connected for this flow. No legal service or COPPA certification purchase has been included.
- Optional dedicated support mailbox at Porkbun: $3/month billed annually ($36 upfront). Existing suitable email may avoid this cost. https://porkbun.com/products/domains
- Backups: storage/provider not selected. Reserve $0–5/month as a planning allowance, not a verified vendor price. The free end assumes you supply suitable existing encrypted off-device storage and perform the required retention/restore work. HTTPS is supplied by hosting; no separate certificate purchase planned.
- Stripe U.S. domestic online cards: 2.9% + $0.30 per successful payment, plus pay-as-you-go Billing at 0.7% of billing volume. At $9.99/month, approximately $0.66 in these fees; at $99.90/year, approximately $3.90. Other payment methods, international cards, currency conversion, disputes, taxes and optional Stripe services can add fees. https://stripe.com/pricing and https://stripe.com/billing/pricing

Base hosting plus standard domain is approximately $98.08 for year one ($87 + $11.08), or $8.17/month averaged across the year. First month plus a full year domain is approximately $18.33. Adding a prepaid support mailbox raises year-one base to $134.08 and initial outlay to approximately $54.33, before backups and payment fees.

A practical initial infrastructure allowance is about $10–20/month, plus transaction fees. The $7 server has limited memory and has not been load-tested with many families; growth may require more resources. Render plan eligibility/usage charges and GitHub Actions quotas must be confirmed for the account.

Not included: business formation/registration, tax filings or sales-tax tooling, business mailing address/phone if needed, insurance, legal review if later chosen, your existing development subscription, support/consent-review labor, or costs of replacing a provider if its terms do not support the children's service. These depend on the final operator and jurisdiction, so an honest all-in total is not yet known.
